1. ABOUT THE COLLEGE
------------------------------------------------------------
Our institution offers undergraduate engineering programmes in
Computer Science, Electronics, Mechanical, Civil, and
Information Technology. The college is affiliated with a
recognised state technical university and approved by AICTE.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
------------------------------------------------------------
To be eligible for admission to any B.Tech programme:

  • Candidates must have passed 10+2 (or equivalent) from a
    recognised board with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics
    (PCM) as core subjects.
  • A minimum aggregate of 60% marks in PCM at the 10+2 level
    is required. SC/ST/OBC candidates receive a 5% relaxation
    (minimum 55%).
  • Candidates appearing in their final 10+2 examination in
    the current academic year are also eligible to apply
    provisionally, subject to meeting the cut-off upon result
    declaration.
  • Age limit: Candidates must be at least 17 years of age as
    on 31 December of the admission year.

3. ENTRANCE EXAMINATION & CUTOFFS
------------------------------------------------------------
Admission is primarily based on JEE Main scores.

  • Minimum qualifying percentile: 85th percentile overall in
    JEE Main (Paper 1 — B.E./B.Tech).
  • Branch-wise indicative closing percentiles (2024 reference):
      - Computer Science & Engineering   : 95th percentile
      - Information Technology           : 92nd percentile
      - Electronics & Communication Eng. : 88th percentile
      - Mechanical Engineering           : 85th percentile
      - Civil Engineering                : 85th percentile
  • Candidates who do not have a JEE Main score may apply
    through the college's own entrance test (CET), conducted
    in the last week of May. CET cutoff: 60 out of 100.
  • Lateral entry (2nd year) is available for diploma holders
    with 60% aggregate; no entrance exam required.

5. FEE STRUCTURE
------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Tuition Fees
  • Annual tuition fee: ₹1,95,000 (1.95 LPA) per academic year.
  • The fee is payable in two installments:
      - Semester 1 (July): ₹97,500
      - Semester 2 (January): ₹97,500
  • A one-time admission/registration fee of ₹5,000 is charged
    at the time of first enrollment.
  • Laboratory and library fee: ₹8,000 per year (included in
    the semester bill).

5.2 Hostel Fees (Per Academic Year)
  • Non-AC double-occupancy room: ₹75,000
    (includes room rent, electricity up to 100 units/month,
     and Wi-Fi)
  • AC double-occupancy room    : ₹1,15,000
    (includes room rent, electricity up to 150 units/month,
     and Wi-Fi)
  • Single-occupancy room (non-AC): ₹95,000
  • Mess charges (optional, per semester): ₹18,000
    (covers breakfast, lunch, and dinner on weekdays)
  • Hostel security deposit (refundable): ₹10,000

5.3 Other Charges
  • Transport (college bus, per year): ₹20,000 – ₹28,000
    depending on distance zone.
  • Examination fee: ₹1,200 per semester (university mandated).

6. SCHOLARSHIPS & FEE WAIVERS
------------------------------------------------------------
  • Merit scholarship: Top 5% of each batch (by JEE Main
    percentile) receives a 25% tuition fee waiver.
  • Government SC/ST scholarship: Full tuition fee
    reimbursement for eligible students as per state norms.
  • Sports quota: Students with state or national level
    sports achievement may receive up to 50% tuition waiver
    (subject to committee approval).
  • EWS scholarship: 10% fee waiver for Economically Weaker
    Section (EWS) certificate holders.
